Carbide Lamp

Senior 3D Artist Art Test

The brief was to model a low-poly carbide lamp from photo references and deliver a normal map and an ambient occlusion map. I delivered the asset on time and met all technical requirements.

Technical requirements

Geometry
Up to 5,000 triangles
Texture set
One set at 2048 × 2048
Normal Map
OpenGL
AO Map
No floor occlusion
Backface culling
Enabled
Height
20 cm
UV layout
One 0–1 tile

Photo references

I used photographs from three angles to reconstruct the body proportions, reflector shape, and smaller details.

Subdivision modeling and high-poly

I built the mid- and high-poly forms using subdivision surface modeling and refined the details in ZBrush. The first two images show the high-poly model in ZBrush, followed by views of the untextured model.

Low-poly, UVs, and baking

I used the same workflow as in my first case study. I retopologized the model in 3ds Max, packed the UVs into one tile in RizomUV, and baked the normal and AO maps in Marmoset Toolbag. During retopology, I focused on keeping the reflector and handle silhouettes intact within the 5,000-triangle budget.

Textures and final renders

After submitting the test, I textured the model and created extra renders for my portfolio. I added scuffs, metal oxidation, and other signs of use to the materials.
