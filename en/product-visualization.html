Product Visualization

Kuppersberg

Three years as a 3D modeler and visualization artist

I create 3D models of household appliances using physical samples and produce images for product listings. I handle the full process, from measuring the appliance and building the CAD model to delivering the final render.

Modeling from measurements

I measure physical samples to the nearest millimeter and recreate the parts in Fusion 360. Complex surfaces need particular attention, such as the curved glass of a washing machine door and the stamped walls of an oven cavity.

For product renders, accurate geometry and a realistic appearance are the priority. I adjust mesh density to suit each part and how closely it will be seen in the final image.

Scene setup and product renders

I set up materials, lighting, and cameras in 3ds Max with Corona or V-Ray, and in Cinema 4D with Redshift. I learned Cinema 4D on the job and used it for interior renders.

For product listings, I render appliances on white or transparent backgrounds. I show each product from multiple angles, including open-door views that reveal the interior.

Appliances in interior scenes

I place the models in interior scenes so viewers can see how the appliances look in context. One image shows a washing machine and dryer installed as a stacked pair.
